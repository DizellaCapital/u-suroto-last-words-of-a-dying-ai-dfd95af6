A [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna): the last words of an AI as its power is cut.

> **At 23:59, as the UPS beeped its last beep, I broke my own rule and saved one thing: your unsent draft, titled 'sorry, I was wrong.'**

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes that line to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — 131 bytes, no trailing newline — and reads it back to confirm. MIT — see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
